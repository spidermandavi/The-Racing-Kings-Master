Titles

All official Racing Kings titles, their requirements, and current holders.

Universal requirements
  • Lichess account must be at least 3 months old.
  • Every norm requires at least 10 games.
  • Winning the tournament is not required for a norm.
  • Blitz is exactly 3+0, 3+2, or 5+0.
  • A standard norm is a qualifying Racing Kings tournament using any other time control.
  • Norm performance threshold equals the title threshold, except RKSGM = 2450.
