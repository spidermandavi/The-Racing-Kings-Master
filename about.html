About

A community-driven ranking and title system for Racing Kings players on Lichess.

Not affiliated with Lichess.

Created and maintained by Mysterious_Past. Contact him for suggestions, problems, questions, help with the project, or to apply for a title.

What The Racing Kings Master Offers

The site includes a growing collection of leaderboards for comparing Racing Kings players in different ways, as well as a Hall of Fame celebrating notable achievements and players in the community.

You can register for an account and log in to access account-based features and become part of the site's community. Your account is used to support features that require a personal profile and a signed-in user.

A dedicated Racing Kings studying section is also coming soon, with the goal of giving players a dedicated place for studying and improving their Racing Kings skills.

Titles and Other Title Systems

The title requirements used here are also recognized and used by the other Racing Kings title system at WRKFED. WRKFED is another option for earning the same Racing Kings titles, provided by a different title-system provider.

Help Is Welcome

The Racing Kings Master is a community-driven project, and help is always welcome. Contributions, ideas, suggestions, feedback, bug reports, and other forms of support can all help the project grow and improve.
